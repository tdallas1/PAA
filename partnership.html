Types of Partnerships

Discover the many ways we can collaborate to serve children and families

Healthcare Organizations

Partner with hospitals, clinics, and health systems to provide direct service opportunities and educational programming for our student advocates.

Partnership Examples:

  • Student volunteer programs in pediatric units
  • Health education workshops for families
  • Community health screening events
  • Mentorship opportunities for pre-health students

Educational Institutions

Collaborate with K-12 schools, universities, and educational nonprofits to create impactful learning experiences that benefit both students and children.

Partnership Examples:

  • After-school tutoring and mentoring programs
  • Health and wellness curriculum development
  • College readiness workshops
  • STEM education initiatives

Community Organizations

Work alongside nonprofits, community centers, and advocacy groups to address the broader social determinants that impact children's health and wellbeing.

Partnership Examples:

  • Food security and nutrition programs
  • Housing stability initiatives
  • Community resource fairs
  • Social justice advocacy campaigns

Corporate & Foundation Partners

Engage businesses and foundations in our mission through funding partnerships, employee volunteer programs, and corporate social responsibility initiatives.

Partnership Examples:

  • Sponsored community health events
  • Employee volunteer days
  • Scholarship and grant funding
  • Professional development workshops

How We Collaborate

Our step-by-step process for building meaningful partnerships

1

Initial Connection

Reach out through our partnership form or contact us directly. We'll schedule a conversation to learn about your organization's mission, current initiatives, and how we might work together to serve children and families.

2

Needs Assessment

Together, we'll identify specific needs, goals, and opportunities for collaboration. We'll discuss your organization's capacity, our students' skills and interests, and how to create mutually beneficial programming.

3

Program Design

We'll co-create programming that aligns with both organizations' goals. This includes developing clear objectives, timelines, volunteer training protocols, and evaluation metrics to measure our collective impact.

4

Implementation & Support

Launch the partnership with ongoing communication and support. We provide trained student volunteers, regular check-ins, and flexibility to adapt programming based on what we learn together.

5

Evaluation & Growth

Regularly assess our impact together, celebrate successes, and identify opportunities to expand or enhance our collaboration. We believe in continuous improvement and long-term relationship building.
