What We Stand For

The Pediatric Advocacy Alliance (PAA) is a student-led organization at Dominican University dedicated to advancing the health, rights, and well-being of children—especially those in underserved communities.

Our mission is to promote pediatric equity through advocacy, education, and service. We empower students to take action on the issues affecting children and families by creating meaningful volunteer opportunities, raising awareness of systemic disparities, and collaborating with partners across healthcare, education, and social services.

Our Pillars

The core values that guide everything we do.

Advocacy

Speaking up for the health, rights, and well-being of children, especially those in underserved communities.

Education

Providing resources, training, and information to families, caregivers, and future change-makers and healthcare professionals.

Outreach

Engaging directly with communities through events, workshops, volunteering, and partnerships.

Empowerment

Equipping students, children and families with the knowledge and tools to create positive, lasting change for children's health and rights.
